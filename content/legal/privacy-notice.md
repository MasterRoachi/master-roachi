| **Electronic marketing** (email, WhatsApp, SMS or phone) after you agree | **Your consent** (POPIA s69(1)(a), s11(1)(a)), only on the channel you chose and only for website design, hosting and website maintenance |

| Providing the services to clients, invoicing and support | The contract with you (POPIA s11(1)(b)) |

| Keeping the "do not contact" list, so we never contact you again after a no | Our legal duty to keep such a list (Information Regulator Guidance Note on Direct Marketing §6.4, §7.3.5) and our legitimate interest in complying (POPIA s11(1)(f)) |

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for any purpose that is not compatible with the ones above (POPIA s13, s15).

## 6. How long we keep it (POPIA s14)

| Information | How long |
|---|---|
| Prospects we have not approached, or who have not consented | **12 months** from our last contact or update, then deleted |
| People who sent an enquiry and did not become clients | **12 months** from our last contact, then deleted; **24 months** if you ticked the box to hear from us. |
| Prospects who said **no**, objected, or did not reply | Details deleted straight away; **only the one-way coded value** (hash) is kept, permanently, so that we never contact you again |
| Prospects who consented but with whom nothing has happened | **24 months** from the last contact, then deleted (the hash is kept) |
| Clients | For the **whole relationship plus 5 years**, for tax and legal purposes |
| Consent records | As long as we may market to you on that consent, and for a reasonable time afterwards to prove it |
| **Call recordings** | **As long as the consent record they support** |

## 7. How we protect it (POPIA s19)

Our system is behind its own separate login. Sensitive values are encrypted, access is limited to us, and we keep backups. No prospect or client information is kept in code repositories. If we believe your information has been accessed by someone unauthorised, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires (POPIA s22).

## 8. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **Know what we hold.** Ask whether we hold information about you, and for a copy (POPIA s23). For a formal request under the Promotion of Access to Information Act (PAIA), email us.
- **Know the source.** Ask where we got your details (POPIA s18(1)(a); ECTA s45(1)(b)).
- **Correct or delete it.** Ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, misleading or unlawfully held (POPIA s24). You may use the prescribed Form 2 of the POPIA Regulations, but an email is fine.
- **Object.** Object at any time to our processing based on legitimate interest, and to direct marketing (POPIA s11(3)). You may use the prescribed Form 1, but any clear message is fine.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time (POPIA s11(2)(b)). This does not affect anything we did before you withdrew.
- **Get a copy or transcript of a call recording** of your consent call, free of charge (POPIA Regulations reg 6.2).
- **Complain to the Information Regulator** (POPIA s74).

To stop all contact from us, tell us **in any way**:
- reply "**stop**" to any email, WhatsApp or SMS from us;
- email roachi@masterroachi.com;
- call or WhatsApp 076 389 4279;
- tell us in person.

It is free (CPA s11(5)). We act on it **the same day**: we stop all marketing, confirm once without any marketing, and add you to our "do not contact" list. We will not contact you again for marketing (POPIA s11(3)–(4); CPA s11(2), s11(4); ECTA s45).
