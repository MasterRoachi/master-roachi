> Outline only. Notes as the reading goes, rather than a review written after.

*On the Incarnation* is short enough to finish in an afternoon and dense enough
that doing so wastes it. These are notes taken at reading pace.

## Why this one first

It is the usual recommendation for a reason, and the reason is worth saying out
loud rather than repeating as advice.

## The argument in his own order

Athanasius builds in a sequence. Summaries rearrange it into something tidier
and lose the part where each step is forced by the last.

## Where it is hardest

The passages that need a second and third pass, and what made them land.

## What it assumes you already believe

Reading a fourth-century text as though it were written for a modern reader is
the quickest way to misread it.
