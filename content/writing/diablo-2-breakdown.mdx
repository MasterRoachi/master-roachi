> Outline only. The argument is sketched, the writing is not done.

Diablo II is thirty years of ARPG design in one game, and most of what came
after is a footnote to it. This is an attempt to say why, system by system,
rather than reaching for nostalgia.

## The loot loop

What the drop tables actually do, why rare items feel rare, and how the game
avoids the modern trap of showering the player with things that do not matter.

## Rune words

The best crafting system in the genre, and the one almost nobody has copied
properly. Notes on why scarcity is the whole point.

## Skill trees that commit

Respec-free builds as a design position rather than an oversight, and what is
lost when a game lets you undo every choice.

## Where it does not hold up

Not a defence. The inventory, the mule runs, the parts that were tedious then
and are tedious now.
