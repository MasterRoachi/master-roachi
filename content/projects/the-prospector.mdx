Estate agents spend their days finding people who might sell — on the phone,
and walking streets door to door. The Prospector keeps that work in one place
for a small agency’s agents: who to call next, what was said, and which houses
on a street have already had a knock.

<Split src="/projects/the-prospector-a.webp" side="right">

It is careful about one thing above everything else. It only lets an agent
contact someone in the way that person has agreed to be contacted.
