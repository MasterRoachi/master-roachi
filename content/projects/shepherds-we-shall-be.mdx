A story about a shepherd who finds something he does not understand, and sets
out to protect the people he loves. It turns out to be harder, and a great deal
more awesome, than he thought it would be.

It starts smaller than that. Eli is looking for a sheep he lost in York.

## The world after

Three generations ago a sickness everyone calls the zombie plague brought the
old world down. What is left is quiet country: flocks, small towns, and ruins
nobody goes into. People have stopped asking what really happened. They were
told, and that was enough.

<Split src="/projects/shepherds-we-shall-be-a.webp" side="right">

Then Eli finds the orb. It has been asleep for a long time, and when it
wakes it can hear something — a signal, calling from somewhere nothing should
be able to call from.

Following it means leaving home.

</Split>

## The people you meet

Eli does not go far alone. Along the road he gathers companions who have no
business travelling together, and the fights are theirs as much as his. Four of
them fight at once, and you can take over any one of them mid-fight rather than
watching the other three get on with it.

What they face is worse than anything the old stories warned about. Some of it
was built. Some of it was not built by anyone.

## Where it is

The first chapter is built, from the morning in York onward, and the rest is
coming a piece at a time. The art is drawn by hand, with Timon.

It is set in [Terrath](/projects/terrath/), the same world as
[The Lost Sheep](/projects/the-lost-sheep/) and
[Cultus Botanicus](/projects/cultus-botanicus/). There is no release date, and
there will not be one until there is something worth putting a date on.
