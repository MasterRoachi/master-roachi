A story about a post-apocalyptic shepherd who finds something he does not
understand, and sets out to protect the people he loves. It turns out to be
harder, and a great deal more awesome, than he thought it would be.

It starts smaller than that. He is looking for a sheep he lost in York.

## What it plays like

<Split src="/projects/shepherds-we-shall-be-a.webp" side="right">

Diablo II meets Breath of Fire III, with AI and demonic robots.

The loot and the fighting come from the first. The party and the story come
from the second — four heroes active at once, every one of them directly
playable, and you switch between them mid-fight rather than watching three
capable people get on with it without you.

</Split>

## Where it is

Built solo in Godot, at roughly five hours a week, and set in
[Terrath](/projects/terrath/) — the same world as
[Cultus Botanicus](/projects/cultus-botanicus/), about a century and a half
apart.

Every milestone has to still be playable before the next one starts, which is
slower than it sounds. There is no release date, and there will not be one
until there is something worth putting a date on.
