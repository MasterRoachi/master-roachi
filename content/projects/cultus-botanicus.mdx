A reboot of *Plant Tycoon*, the 2007 plant-breeding sim. You grow plants,
cross-pollinate them, and hunt for the rare hybrids hiding in the combination
space, while keeping the ordinary ones alive long enough to fund the search.

The original's hook was patience: a garden is a slow system, and the game
respected that instead of rushing you. Rebuilding it is partly an excuse to
work out how much of that slowness was the point and how much was just 2007.
