The world is nearly empty. Somewhere in it, a thousand people are still alive.

You are the mayor of York, and what you have is a radio. You broadcast, and
survivors hear you and walk in — with names and skills you did not choose — and
you put them to work rebuilding.

<Split src="/projects/the-lost-sheep-a.webp" side="right">

You win the island back a piece at a time, always arguing with yourself over
whether the next thing should be another house or everything else. Some people
will not come on their own, so you send parties out to bring them in.

You get older. One day you die, and your child takes over the broadcasts.

</Split>

It ends when you have found all thousand. You cannot lose: playing badly costs
you time, never the town.

## Where it fits

It is set in [Terrath](/projects/terrath/), in the years before
[Shepherds We Shall Be](/projects/shepherds-we-shall-be/). It is small on
purpose — meant to be the first of these games to be finished.

## Where it is

The game underneath works: the town, the broadcasts, the people walking in.
The art and the story are still to come, and Timon will be drawing it.
