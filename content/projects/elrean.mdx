You are one of the Elrean, and your hand is in the earth.

The land comes in steps and layers. You lift it, spread it and flatten it, and
a people find the ground you shaped and settle there. Give them land, food,
shelter and something to wonder at, and they believe. Their belief is what lets
you do more.

## A hand that waits to be asked

You act only when your people ask you to. So your reach is only as wide as
their trust, and it grows as they do. You make nothing out of nothing:
you move the earth that is already there.

When they ask, you send rain, fire, healing and storms. You can lift a person
and set them somewhere safer, move a rock or a tree or a house, and speak
through a prophet. The land answers back, with water finding its way down,
weather, seasons, and old things buried under it waiting to be dug out.

## Neighbours

Not everyone on the land is yours. Other tribes serve the fallen, who are the
gods of the nations, and you can win them over with gifts, miracles and trade,
or meet them in war.

Your people grow from a tribe to towns and kingdoms, and on to the first
machines and steam. Things go wrong for them, but you cannot lose. There are
setbacks, never a game over.

## Where it fits

It is set in [Terrath](/projects/terrath/), the same world as
[Shepherds We Shall Be](/projects/shepherds-we-shall-be/),
[The Lost Sheep](/projects/the-lost-sheep/) and
[Cultus Botanicus](/projects/cultus-botanicus/). When in Terrath's history it
takes place is not settled yet.

## Where it is

The first piece is built: a toy for shaping the land, with a handful of people
who climb it and settle wherever it is flat. It comes first because if shaping
the land does not feel good, nothing else matters. The eras, the miracles and
the neighbours come after it. There is no release date.
