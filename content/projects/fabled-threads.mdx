An online print-on-demand store. The merch is built around original
illustrations drawn in the register of anime and early-2000s cartoons — the
Saturday-morning end of the spectrum rather than the prestige end.

Print-on-demand means no inventory and no minimum runs: a design goes up, and
it gets printed when someone orders it. That keeps the risk on the drawing
rather than on a garage full of unsold shirts.
