Imprint is a workshop in Knysna that puts names and badges on things:
embroidery, printing, signs and laser engraving. Most of its work is for
schools and clubs, ordering a lot of the same thing at once.

<Split src="/projects/imprint-knysna-a.webp" side="right">

The site lets a customer choose a garment, say what goes on it, and order — or
get in touch and ask.

</Split>

It is built, and waiting on the workshop’s own prices and details before it
opens.
