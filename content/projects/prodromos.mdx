Pródromos sells icons — made in the monasteries of Greece and on Mount Athos,
and mounted on wood — along with prayer ropes, incense and gifts, from
Pretoria.

<Split src="/projects/prodromos-a.webp" side="right">

The shop holds more than two hundred and fifty icons, and you can look a saint
up by name to find theirs. Delivery is arranged from the shop itself.

</Split>

It is built and on its own address, and the owner is looking it over before it
opens to everyone.
