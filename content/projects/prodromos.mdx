Pródromos is a small Orthodox shop in Pretoria. It sells icons from the
monasteries of Greece and Mount Athos, printed and mounted on wood, as well as
crosses, incense and vigil-lamp pieces. I built its website and online shop.

## The look

An icon belongs in a quiet corner of the house, so I kept the shop quiet too.
Warm paper colours, plenty of room around everything, and headings in the
shop's own typeface, which was made at the Holy Monastery of Gregoriou on
Mount Athos in 1994.

*Pródromos* is Greek for "forerunner", so the home page opens on Saint John
the Forerunner. His icon is shown mounted on its block of wood, the way it
arrives, and you can grab it and turn it to see the edge.

The icons are sorted the way people look for them: Christ, the Panagia, the
angels, the saints and the feasts. After those come the silkscreen icons,
which are imported ready-made, and then everything else the shop sells.
There are over 250 icons, and each has its own page with its sizes and
photographs.

## Buying for a name day

A lot of icons are bought as gifts for someone's name day, and the person
buying usually knows the name but not the saint. So there's a search for that.
Type in *Nikolai*, *Eleni* or *Jovan*, and you get the saint, their feast day
and every icon of them in the shop.

There's also a list of feast days in calendar order, each with a countdown,
so a godparent can see a name day coming and order early. If the shop doesn't
have the icon someone wants, they can ask for it.

## His words and his photographs

The owner wrote the descriptions himself: where the incense comes from (the
Holy Monastery of Dochiariou, on Mount Athos), what each cross is, how
delivery works. His photographs are shown whole instead of being cropped to
fit a grid.

He also has his own admin area. That's where he adds products, sets prices,
uploads photos and sees icon requests as they come in.

## Ordering

For now, orders go through WhatsApp, which is how his customers already talk
to him. The basket turns into a single message with everything in it. Card
payments and courier delivery are built in too, and he'll switch them on when
he's ready.

## Where it stands

The shop is finished and live on its own domain, but it's still behind a
holding page while the owner looks it over. It opens when he's happy with it.
