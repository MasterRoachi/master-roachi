Greg is me. Larry is Timon. Between us we make [Terrath](/projects/terrath/)
and the games set in it, and at some point the software we were using became
the slowest part of the job.

So we started making our own.

<Split src="/projects/greg-and-larrys-tools-a.webp" side="right">

They are built for exactly two people, which is the whole advantage. Nothing
has to please everyone. Each one only has to be good at what we do,
and hand its work straight to the next.

</Split>

None of them are for sale or for download. They are ours. This page is here
because they are a real part of what gets made, not because you can have them.
